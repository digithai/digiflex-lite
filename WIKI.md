# DigiFlex Lite — User Guide

This guide explains how to use DigiFlex Lite as an end user.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Open the application in your browser (e.g., `http://localhost:7091`).
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

If you see "Invalid credentials":

- Make sure Caps Lock is off.
- Confirm you are using the email address provided by your admin.
- If you forgot your password, contact your admin.

---

## 2. Dashboard

After logging in, the dashboard shows:

- Your personal WFH calendar.
- Approved and pending requests.

Use the sidebar to navigate between sections based on your role.

---

## 3. Submitting a WFH Request

Users, approvers, and admins can submit a Work From Home (WFH) request.

1. Go to the **Dashboard**.
2. Select a date for your WFH request.
3. Click **Submit Request**.

The request will appear as **pending** until it is approved or rejected.

**Email notifications:** When you submit a request, approvers receive an email notification. If you are an approver submitting a request, admins are notified instead.

### Request Rules

Admins can configure rules such as:

- How far in advance requests must be made.
- Which weekdays WFH is not allowed.
- Public holidays block requests automatically.

---

## 4. Approving or Rejecting Requests (Approver / Admin)

1. Open **Approvals** from the sidebar.
2. View pending requests grouped by position or team.
3. Review each request.
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.

Approved or rejected requests are updated immediately in the requester's dashboard.

---

## 5. Team Management (Admin)

Admins can manage users in the **Team** section.

### Create a User

1. Click **Create User**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Password
   - Role (`user`, `approver`, or `admin`)
   - Position, team, office, country
   - Weekly WFH allowance
3. Click **Save**.

### Edit a User

- Update WFH weekly allowance.
- Reset password if needed.

### Delete a User

- Find the user in the team list.
- Click **Delete**.
- Note: you cannot delete your own account or a superuser account.

---

## 6. WFH Rules Settings (Admin)

In the **Settings** section, admins can configure:

- **Allowed date scopes**: how far in advance users can request WFH.
- **Disallowed weekdays**: days when WFH is not allowed.
- **Position concurrency**: limits how many people in the same position can take WFH on the same day.

Changes apply immediately to new requests.

---

## 7. Public Holidays (Admin)

Admins can add public holidays in the **Settings** section. Users cannot request WFH on dates marked as holidays.

---

## 8. Logging Out

Click **Logout** in the sidebar to end your session.

---

## Tips

- Submit requests early to avoid conflicts with team limits.
- Contact your admin if your role or permissions look incorrect.
- If you are not receiving email notifications, check with your admin that email is configured.
